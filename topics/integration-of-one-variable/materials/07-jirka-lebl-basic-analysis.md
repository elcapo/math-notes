# Jiří Lebl — Basic Analysis I

Why this material: free, rigorous undergraduate real-analysis textbook (Volume I covers reals, sequences, series, continuity, derivatives, the **Riemann integral**, and metric spaces). Chapter 5 develops the integral via Darboux upper/lower sums and proves the equivalence with the Riemann-sum definition, which is the gap left by the MIT 18.01 notes. Used as the reference text when a proof is needed at the level of `Analyse I`.
