# MIT 18.01 — Lecture 20: Second Fundamental Theorem

Why this material: the second half of the FTC, $\frac{d}{dx}\int_a^x f(t)\,dt = f(x)$, which establishes the integral-as-function-of-its-upper-limit and closes the duality with differentiation. Provides existence of antiderivatives for continuous functions — the conceptual climax of the integration unit.
