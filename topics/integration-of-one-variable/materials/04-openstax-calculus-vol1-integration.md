# OpenStax Calculus Volume 1 — chapters 5 and 6

Why this material: textbook companion to the MIT lectures. Chapter 5 covers the Riemann integral, the FTC, substitution, and integration by parts; chapter 6 covers areas between curves, volumes, work, and arc length. Reference only — consult when a topic from the MIT lectures needs more worked examples or alternative phrasing.
