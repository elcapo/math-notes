# SpetzoMath — Riemann (…Darboux) Integrals

Why this material: 43-minute lecture-style video that defines Darboux integrability on a bounded interval and proves the integrability criterion "$\exists P$ with $U(f,P) - L(f,P) < \varepsilon$". It then disentangles the often-conflated terms *Riemann integrable* and *Darboux integrable* — exactly the point §1.2–§1.3 of the theory leaves implicit. Part of a larger real-analysis playlist; useful to deepen §1 before moving on to `Analyse I`.
