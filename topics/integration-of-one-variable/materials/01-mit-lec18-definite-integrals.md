# MIT 18.01 — Lecture 18: Definite Integrals

Why this material: the entry point to integration as an *area-under-the-curve* limit of Riemann sums. Sets up the definite integral $\int_a^b f(x)\,dx$ rigorously before the fundamental theorem of calculus arrives in the next two lectures.
